# Variations and mixing from the canvas latent (prototype, 2026-10-31)

Made by the "variations" designer of the design workflow (state/CURRENT.md, 2026-10-31 15:30) from the existing
canvas latent `vt-v3-d02-s5` and its flow prior `lfp-v2x-exploratory` (no training): `vary.png` shows variations
around one emoji per row at increasing strength (left: the input); `mix.png` and `mix2.png` mix two emoji through
the latent. `check.py` and `check2.py` are the scripts that drew them, kept as written.

Where the corpus is dense - busts, faces, couples with a heart, stick figures - the variations are plausible
(other hair, skin, beard, expression) and faces mix into new faces. Elsewhere (signs, glasses, swimmers, desks,
robots) they fragment. Not scored against criteria; kept as material for the art-installation ideas.
