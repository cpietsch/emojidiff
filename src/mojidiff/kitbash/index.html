Kitbash

Take parts from two OpenMoji icons, place and layer them on the same 72-unit box, and get the program back through the project's codec. Everything here is vector: the parts are the icons' own paths, and the export is the canonical SVG. Drag to move, wheel or +/- to scale, f to flip, [ ] to change layer, Delete to remove.
