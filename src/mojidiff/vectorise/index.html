Re-vectorise

Pick an OpenMoji icon the model never trained on, paint on it, and a small render-to-SVG model transcribes the pixels back into an editable SVG program. The time shown is the model's own, on the RTX 4080, from pixels to a validated program.

Pick an icon to start.

Every output is a valid program in the project's codec: at most 32 paths and 128 segments, OpenMoji's palette and stroke widths. Colours are snapped to that palette.
