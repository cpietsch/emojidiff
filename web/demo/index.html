How this is measured

ms per icon runs from the Uint8 pixels to the SVG text: the encoder, every decoder call (one per step whose grammar mask leaves a choice), the JavaScript grammar, the masked argmax and the codec. The model download and session creation are not in it. The first icon after loading includes one-off warm-up and stays out of the median and p90; your own images stay out of all the tiles. The 4 WASM threads and WebGPU need a secure context (https or localhost); over plain http the browser gets 1 WASM thread and no WebGPU. On the RTX 4080 workstation in Chrome the median was 1,171 ms with 4 WASM threads and 1,519 ms with one.
